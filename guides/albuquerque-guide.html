Road Trip Stop — Albuquerque, NM

A high-desert city shaped by two federal projects it never asked for — the Manhattan Project's Sandia Labs and the interstate/Route 66 corridor — and increasingly defined by television: Breaking Bad and Better Call Saul turned its car washes and drive-ins into pilgrimage sites.

✦ Pop-Culture Connections

Films Shot Here

  • Breaking Bad (2008–2013) — Vince Gilligan's Albuquerque-set-and-shot saga. The Whites' house (private, exterior only — please don't re-enact the pizza scene), Twisters (Los Pollos Hermanos), The Dog House Drive-In, and a real car wash all still stand; the Crossroads Motel's status is unconfirmed.
  • Better Call Saul (2015–2022) — the prequel used the Alvarado Transportation Center for Mike Ehrmantraut's Rail Runner arrival; still a working transit hub, freely visitable.
  • El Camino: A Breaking Bad Movie (2019) — Netflix's follow-up reused much of the parent series' ABQ location library.
  • Terminator Salvation (2009) — large-scale action sequences shot in and around Albuquerque Studios, part of what built the city into a major production hub pre-Netflix.

Notable People Born Here

  • Neil Patrick Harris (b. 1973) — actor, host, Broadway performer; born in ABQ, raised in Ruidoso, NM.
  • Jeff Bezos (b. 1964) — born in Albuquerque; his grandfather ran the city's Atomic Energy Commission office, tying his birthplace to the same federal-land history as the Nuclear Science Museum below. Included as friction against this traveler's politics, not celebration.
  • Jay Roach — director of the Austin Powers films and Meet the Parents; also directed Trumbo (2015), about blacklisted screenwriter Dalton Trumbo.
  • Paula Gunn Allen (b. 1939, Cubero, NM) — Indigenous feminist poet and scholar (The Sacred Hoop, 1986); closely tied to Albuquerque/UNM's literary community though born just outside city limits.

Correction: Breaking Bad's pilot and several cook-site sequences were shot well outside city limits, near the To'hajiilee Indian Reservation and other Bernalillo County high-desert sites — not every "Breaking Bad location" a tour operator names is actually in Albuquerque proper. — Sources: breakingbad-locations.com, KRQE
