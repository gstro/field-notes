Road Trip Stop — Boise, ID

A frontier capital built partly by its own inmates, home to the largest Basque diaspora community in the Americas and the unlikely birthplace of both foundational hardcore punk and '90s indie rock.

✦ Pop-Culture Connections

Films Shot Here

  • Bronco Billy (1980) — Clint Eastwood dark comedy-western, bar fights and a train robbery shot around town.
  • Moving (1988) — Richard Pryor comedy shot on Boise streets, about a transport engineer relocating to the city.
  • Always (1989) — Spielberg's aerial-firefighter romance, largely shot in Boise National Forest just outside the city.
  • Ghost Dad (1990) — Bill Cosby comedy, partially filmed in Boise.

Notable People Born / Based Here

  • Frank Church — U.S. Senator born in Boise (1924); chaired the Church Committee that exposed CIA/FBI/NSA surveillance abuses, foundational to modern civil-liberties politics.
  • Doug Martsch — formed Built to Spill in Boise in 1992; locals credit him with raising the city's whole music profile.
  • Brian "Pushead" Schroeder — Septic Death's vocalist and a legendary hardcore/metal illustrator (Metallica, Misfits).
  • Joëlle Jones — comics artist/writer (Lady Killer, Catwoman), born in Boise.

DIY Electronics & Maker Spaces

Honest gap (Bookstores): the mandatory anarchist/infoshop/worker-owned bookstore search found no storefront in Boise. Boise DSA is active and organizes locally, but it's a working group, not a physical space — the city's radical-left infrastructure runs through organizing rather than a retail counterspace, unlike Salt Lake City's Boing! Collective.
Honest gap (DIY Electronics): Boise has no dedicated synth shop or hackerspace with published public open-hours, unlike Salt Lake City's Squarewave Sound and 801 Labs. Maker Shop Boise is an excellent 7,000 sq ft facility, but it's woodworking, not electronics — padding this category to five with it would misrepresent the city. Four honest picks instead of five.
