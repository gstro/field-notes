Road Trip Stop — Dallas, TX

A city that rebuilt itself twice on erasure — Freedman's Town paved for a freeway, Deep Ellum's Black jazz corridor gutted by the same highway era, then re-grown decades later into a DIY punk scene in the same abandoned warehouses.

✦ Pop-Culture Connections

Films Shot Here

  • RoboCop (1987) — Dallas City Hall's I.M. Pei inverted-pyramid building doubled as OCP Headquarters and the Detroit PD exterior. Free to view from the plaza, 1500 Marilla St.
  • JFK (1991) — Oliver Stone shot on the actual Dealey Plaza assassination site (411 Elm St); the grassy knoll and the Texas School Book Depository are free to walk today.
  • Dallas Buyers Club (2013) — Texas Theatre (231 W Jefferson Blvd, Oak Cliff), the same restored 1931 theater where Lee Harvey Oswald was captured hours after the assassination.
  • Bottle Rocket (1996) — Wes Anderson's directorial debut, shot around Dallas where he grew up.
⚠ Commonly misattributed: the TV series Dallas (1978–91)'s signature location, Southfork Ranch, is actually in Parker, TX — about 25 miles north of downtown, not within city limits.

Notable People Born Here

  • Stevie Ray Vaughan — blues guitar legend, raised in Oak Cliff; a statue stands in Kiest Park.
  • Erykah Badu — neo-soul icon raised in South Dallas, in the shadow of Freedman's Town's former footprint; explicitly political lyrics, active local community ties.
  • Dimebag Darrell — Pantera guitarist, DFW's biggest contribution to heavy/hardcore-adjacent music.
  • Gibby Haynes — Butthole Surfers frontman, noise-punk provocateur from the Texas underground.
  • Lee Harvey Oswald — included as unavoidable, load-bearing Dallas history, not an endorsement: assassinated JFK from the Book Depository, captured at the Texas Theatre.
