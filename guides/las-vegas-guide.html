Road Trip Stop — Las Vegas, NV

Underneath the Strip is a real desert city: a converted-warehouse arts district, the strongest union local in the country, a hackerspace, and a museum Fat Mike from NOFX built out of Kurt Cobain's couch. This guide routes around the casino floor.
10 categories · 5 picks each (+ bonus sights) · Sources: Atlas Obscura, TasteAtlas, Wikipedia, Rebel Atlas, Vegas 411, local press

✦ Pop-Culture Connections

Films Shot Here

  • Casino (1995) — Scorsese's crew used Atomic Liquors' garage as a production studio; the demolished Riviera and the La Concha Motel (now the Neon Museum's visitor center) both appear on screen.
  • Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas (1998) — Graceland Wedding Chapel is a filming location; Circus Circus refused access so its "hotel" was built on a soundstage.
  • Ocean's Eleven (2001) — the Bellagio's fountains, gallery, and Chihuly-glass lobby ceiling are the primary location.
  • Anora (2024) — Palme d'Or and Best Picture winner (Sean Baker); filmed at the Palms Casino Resort and on Fremont Street.
  • The Hangover (2009) / Mars Attacks! (1996) — both used Atomic Liquors and the Neon Museum boneyard, respectively.

Notable People Born / Raised Here

  • Jenna Jameson — born 1974, Las Vegas; daughter of a Folies Bergère showgirl, began dancing in Vegas clubs as a teenager.
  • Brandon Flowers (The Killers) — born Henderson, NV, 1981; Sam's Town and Battle Born are explicit odes to Nevada.
  • Brendon Urie (Panic! at the Disco) — born/raised Summerlin, Las Vegas; the band formed among Summerlin high schoolers.
  • Dan Reynolds (Imagine Dragons) — born Las Vegas, 1987; the band relocated to and was based in his hometown.
  • Pre-Killers Vegas scene — per Wikipedia, the local scene the Killers broke out of "was dominated by punk, nu metal, and rap bands."

⚠ Current Status Notes:
The Golden Gate Hotel's original 50¢ shrimp cocktail — the dish that made this a Vegas cliché — was discontinued in 2017 when Du-par's closed; owner Derek Stevens briefly revived it in 2019 and it now lives on at his Circa Resort instead. Cowtown Guitars, once a nationally known vintage-guitar shop in the downtown Arts District, closed in 2019 — it's listed below for context only, not as a visitable pick. Punk Rock Bowling festival falls in late May, well outside this trip's Oct 9–13 window — listed as a scene-context citation, not a bookable October activity.
Honest gap: unlike Salt Lake City (Squarewave Sound) or Oklahoma City (Keeley Electronics), Las Vegas does not appear to have a currently operating dedicated synth/pedal boutique or electronics-surplus storefront — repeated searches surfaced only national chains. SYN Shop is the one genuine, verifiable DIY-electronics anchor in the city for a metro this size. Likewise, the mandatory anarchist/infoshop bookstore search turned up no Vegas storefront; the city's leftist political infrastructure runs through the Culinary Workers Union Local 226 instead of a bookstore-anchored infoshop scene.
