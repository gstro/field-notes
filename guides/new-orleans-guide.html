✦ Pop-Culture Connections

Films Shot Here

  • Interview with the Vampire (1994) — Anne Rice's French Quarter/Lafayette Cemetery No. 1 Gothic atmosphere anchors the film's New Orleans identity.
  • American Horror Story: Coven (2013) — LaLaurie Mansion exterior (1140 Royal St, private residence), Lafayette Cemetery No. 1, Hermann-Grima House.
  • 12 Years a Slave (2013) — Audubon Park, the Columns Hotel, and the French Quarter.
  • NCIS: New Orleans (2014–2021) — Café Du Monde, Jackson Square, Bourbon Street, Louis Armstrong Park.
  • The Curious Case of Benjamin Button (2008) — the Nolan House on Coliseum St, Garden District; Lake Pontchartrain.

Notable People Born Here

  • Louis Armstrong — jazz's foundational figure, born and raised here; Congo Square and Armstrong Park carry the legacy.
  • Anne Rice — Gothic/horror novelist (The Vampire Chronicles), born in the Irish Channel, 1941.
  • Truman Capote — born at Touro Infirmary, 1924; Southern Gothic literary lineage.
  • Jelly Roll Morton — composer/pianist who claimed to have "invented jazz" in 1902.
  • Phil Anselmo — hardcore-adjacent metal vocalist (Pantera, Down, Superjoint), New Orleans native.
