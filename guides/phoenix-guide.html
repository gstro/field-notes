Road Trip Stop — Phoenix, AZ

The desert capital where the chimichanga's disputed invention, Cesar Chavez's 1972 hunger strike, and the birth of American skate-punk all happened within a few miles of each other.
10 categories · 5 picks each · Sources: Atlas Obscura, TasteAtlas, Eater, Timeout, Phoenix New Times, targeted web search

✦ Pop-Culture Connections

Films Shot Here

  • Psycho (1960) — Alfred Hitchcock's opening sequence, Marion Crane's office scene, was shot in downtown Phoenix.
  • Raising Arizona (1987) — Coen Brothers classic shot around Phoenix and Tempe.
  • Wayne's World (1992) — Partly shot in Phoenix, featuring a cameo from Phoenix-rooted Alice Cooper.
  • The Nutty Professor (1963) — Prom scene shot at ASU's Frank Lloyd Wright–designed Gammage Auditorium in Tempe.
  • Little Miss Sunshine (2006) — Arizona road-trip sequences.

Notable People Born / Raised Here

  • Alice Cooper — raised in Phoenix from childhood (born Detroit); godfather of shock/horror-rock, founded his original band here in 1964, still owns Alice Cooperstown restaurant downtown.
  • Chester Bennington — born Phoenix, 1976; Linkin Park frontman, alt-metal.
  • Stevie Nicks — born Phoenix, 1948; Fleetwood Mac.
  • Brian Brannon / JFA — formed Jodie Foster's Army in Phoenix, 1981; foundational hardcore/skate-punk band; Brannon later became Thrasher magazine's music editor.
  • Meat Puppets — formed in Phoenix/Tempe; underground rock pioneers famously covered by Nirvana on MTV Unplugged.

Next Steps

Deeper Dive JFA (Jodie Foster's Army), formed in Phoenix in 1981, fused surf rock and hardcore into skate-punk — frontman Brian Brannon later became Thrasher magazine's music editor. Worth its own entry alongside Meat Puppets and Sun City Girls as Phoenix's underappreciated underground-music lineage.
Political / Historical Thread Santa Rita Hall's 1972 Cesar Chavez fast and the coining of "Sí, se puede" is a direct Southwest labor-organizing counterpart to the civil-rights and surveillance-politics threads surfacing elsewhere on this trip — worth flagging for a cross-trip labor/organizing thread as the route continues into Albuquerque.
