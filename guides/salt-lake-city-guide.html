Road Trip Stop — Salt Lake City, UT

A planned Mormon pioneer capital where coffee is culturally taboo yet the city seeded Western specialty roasting; the "Beehive State's" literal bee iconography; and a labor-history thread running from IWW founder Bill Haywood's birth here in 1869 through fellow Wobbly Joe Hill's 1915 execution at what's now Sugar House Park.

✦ Pop-Culture Connections

Films Shot Here

  • SLC Punk! (1998) — James Merendino's dramatization of the city's real 1980s punk scene; West High, U of U's Presidents Circle, and the Cathedral of the Madeleine are all still standing (Cottonwood Mall, sadly, is demolished).
  • High School Musical (2006) — filmed at real, still-operating East High School; tours available if you call ahead.
  • The Sandlot (1993) — the dirt lot on Glenrose Drive in the Glendale neighborhood is still there, on private property; view from the street.
  • Dumb and Dumber (1994) — set in Aspen, CO, but almost entirely filmed around the Wasatch Front (Park City's Main Street stands in for Aspen).

Notable People Born Here

  • Bill Haywood — founding IWW member, born in Salt Lake City in 1869; see the Sights section for this guide's political-thread anchor.
  • Neal Cassady — Beat Generation figure, born SLC 1926, inspired Dean Moriarty in Kerouac's On the Road.
  • Natacha Rambova — silent-film costume/art director (and Valentino's wife) turned serious Egyptologist and occult writer — a genuine film-meets-esoterica crossover.
  • Roseanne Barr — comedian and creator/star of Roseanne; the well-known/must-mention pop-culture name on this list.
